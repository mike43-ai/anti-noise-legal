# Delete Your Account & Data

This page explains how to delete your **Anti Noise** account and the data associated with it. It applies to the Anti Noise apps on iOS and Android.

## Delete from inside the app

1. Open **Anti Noise** and make sure you are signed in.
2. Go to the **Profile** tab.
3. Scroll to the **Account** section and tap **Delete account**.
4. Confirm when prompted.

Your account is deleted right away. There is no separate request to wait on.

## What gets deleted

When you delete your account, we permanently remove:

- Your account record with our authentication provider (email, display name, account identifier).
- All content stored on your device (captures, AI summaries, flashcards, learning progress, streaks, and preferences).
- Your learning preferences and profile used to personalize content.

This action is **permanent and cannot be undone.**

## What is not retained

Our AI processing is stateless — captures sent for summarization are not stored on our servers after the response is returned. We do not sell your data or use your content to train models.

Anonymous, non-identifying diagnostic and crash data that cannot be linked back to you may be retained in aggregate.

## Can't access the app?

If you can no longer sign in or open the app, email us and we will delete your account and associated data on your behalf:

**Email:** [support.gravita@gmail.com](mailto:support.gravita@gmail.com)

Please send the request from the email address associated with your account so we can verify it. We will process the deletion within 30 days.

## Export your data first

Before deleting, you can export a copy of your captures and summaries from **Profile → Account → Export my data**.
