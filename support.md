# Anti Noise — Support

Need help with Anti Noise? We're here.

## Contact

**Email:** support.gravita@gmail.com

We typically respond within 1–2 business days.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I cancel my Pro subscription?

Open the **Settings** app on your iPhone → tap your name at the top → **Subscriptions** → **Anti Noise** → **Cancel Subscription**.

Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period; you keep Pro access until then.

### My AI summary failed. What can I do?

Check your internet connection and try again. If the issue persists, send us a screenshot of the error and a brief description of what you captured.

### I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, tap **Forgot Password?** and follow the reset email we send.

### How do I delete my account?

Open **Profile → Account → Delete Account** inside the app. Your data is soft-deleted for 7 days, then permanently removed. Contact us at the email above if you need to restore an account within that window.

### Is my data private?

Yes. We never sell your data. See our [Privacy Policy](/privacy/) for the full breakdown of what we collect and why.

### How do I restore a previous purchase?

Open **Profile → Restore Purchases**. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID that originally bought Pro.

### Which devices does Anti Noise support?

iPhone running iOS 17 or newer. iPad support is planned for a future release.

## Bug Reports and Feature Requests

We read every message. When reporting a bug, please include:

- Your device model and iOS version
- What you were doing when the issue happened
- A screenshot or screen recording if you have one

For feature requests, just tell us the outcome you want — we'll figure out how to build it.
